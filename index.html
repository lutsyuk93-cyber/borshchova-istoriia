A classic homestyle dish

Classic Ukrainian Borscht homemade recipe

A simple recipe for rich, homemade borscht that warms you up, brings the family to the table, and tastes even better the next day.

See ingredients Jump to cooking
⏱ 1 h 40 min • 6 servings • Medium difficulty

A small secret

The best borscht loves a rest

After cooking, let the borscht sit covered for 15–20 minutes. The flavors will deepen.

🥣 Homemade taste in every spoonful

What you'll need

Ingredients

Amounts are calculated for roughly six servings. Feel free to adjust to your taste.

🍖

Base

  • Bone-in meat 500 g
  • Water 2.5 l
  • Bay leaf 2 pcs.
  • Salt to taste
🥕

Vegetables

  • Beet 2 pcs.
  • Potato 4 pcs.
  • Cabbage 300 g
  • Carrot & onion 1 each
🌿

For flavor

  • Tomato paste 2 tbsp
  • Oil 2 tbsp
  • Garlic 2–3 cloves
  • Pepper to taste
💡
Tip

For a vibrant color, splash the beet with a little lemon juice or vinegar.

Step by step

Cooking process

Don't rush — every step helps build a rich flavor and a pleasant aroma.

01

Make the broth

Put the meat in a pot, cover with cold water, and bring to a boil. Skim off the foam, add salt, and simmer for about 60–90 minutes.

02

Prep the vegetables

Dice the potatoes, shred the cabbage. Finely chop the onion, and grate the carrot and beet on a coarse grater.

03

Make the sauté

Sauté the onion and carrot in oil. Add the beet and tomato paste. Pour in a few spoons of broth and simmer 7–10 minutes.

04

Add potatoes and cabbage

Remove the meat from the broth and cut it into pieces. Add the potatoes to the broth, and after 5–7 minutes, add the cabbage.

05

Combine everything

Add the sauté, meat, bay leaf, and pepper. Simmer another 10–15 minutes over low heat.

06

Let it rest

Turn off the heat, add minced garlic, and let the borscht rest covered for 15–20 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

How to make great borscht

Quick answers to questions that often come up while making homemade borscht.

When should I add the beet?

It's easiest to cook the beet separately in the sauté and add it closer to the end of cooking — this helps keep the color vibrant.

How long should borscht rest?

After turning off the heat, let it sit covered for at least 15–20 minutes. If you have time, a longer rest makes the flavor even more pronounced.

How many servings does this recipe make?

The listed amounts are calculated for roughly 6 servings. Use our borscht calculator to scale the ingredients to any number of people.

What should I serve with borscht?

The classic way is with sour cream, fresh herbs, and bread. Garlic buns (pampushky) also make a great side.
