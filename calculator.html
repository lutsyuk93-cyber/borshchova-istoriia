Frequently asked questions

About scaling borscht servings

How much borscht is in one serving?

On average, one serving is about 350–400 ml of finished borscht — a full, deep bowl.

Can I use this calculator for a meat-free version?

Yes — replace the water with vegetable broth and set the meat amount to 0. The rest of the vegetable proportions stay accurate.

What if my pot is smaller than what's needed?

Reduce the number of servings in the calculator to match your pot's volume, or cook in two batches.

Does the amount of spices scale proportionally too?

Salt and pepper are best added to taste in small increments, even if the calculator shows a large number of servings.
