The final step

The borscht is ready — time to serve

Hot, aromatic, and rich. All that's left is a few simple finishing touches to make it look even more appetizing.

See the result Serving options

What the result should look like

Finished borscht

Properly made borscht has a rich color, a pleasant aroma, and tender vegetables that aren't overcooked.

Before serving, check:

  • The broth should be clear and aromatic.
  • Potato and cabbage — tender but not mushy.
  • The beet gives the dish its beautiful red color.
  • The flavor should be balanced: salty, slightly sweet, and a little tangy.
  • The borscht should be hot, but not boiling in the bowl.
🥣

The perfect bowl

Ladle the borscht into a deep bowl, add a spoonful of sour cream, a bit of fresh herbs, and serve with bread or garlic buns.

Pick your style

Ways to serve it

Borscht can be served homestyle, festively, or as a lighter modern version.

🥄

Classic

Top with sour cream, fresh dill, and serve with dark bread.

Sour cream Dill Bread
🧄

With garlic buns

The best option for a family dinner. Rub the buns (pampushky) with garlic and herbs.

Pampushky Garlic Herbs
🌿

Light version

Serve without sour cream, but with plenty of herbs, green onion, and fresh bread.

Green onion Parsley Bread
01

How to plate it nicely

Wipe the rim of the bowl with a clean napkin, pour in the borscht, place the sour cream in the center, and garnish with herbs. Serve right away, while it's hot.
